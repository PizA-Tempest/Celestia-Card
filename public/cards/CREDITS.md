Card artwork
============

The images in this folder are scans of the Rider-Waite-Smith Tarot deck,
illustrated by Pamela Colman Smith and first published by William Rider &
Son in 1909. The artwork is in the public domain worldwide.

Source: Wikimedia Commons — "Category:Rider-Waite-Smith tarot deck (TaionWC)"
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Rider-Waite-Smith_tarot_deck_(TaionWC)

Files are named after card ids in src/data/tarot.json and can be re-fetched
or refreshed at any width via scripts/fetch-rws-artwork.ps1.
